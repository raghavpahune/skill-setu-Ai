# SKILLSETUAI — PROJECT SPECIFICATION

## 1. PROJECT NAME

SkillSetuAI

AI-Powered Labour-Market Intelligence & Curriculum-Alignment Platform


Organization:
Government of Maharashtra

Department:
Maharashtra State Innovation Society —
Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship & Innovation

---

# 2. CORE PROBLEM

Skill-development programs may not stay aligned with changing technologies, local industry demand, emerging job roles, productivity standards and employer expectations.

Course curricula, equipment, trainer capacity and assessment methods can lag behind industry requirements.

This creates:

- Skill mismatch
- Poor placement outcomes
- Outdated courses
- Oversupplied training
- Shortage of emerging skills
- Poor employer satisfaction
- Slow curriculum revision
- Poor district-level training planning
- Unclear career pathways for students

SkillSetuAI must create a continuous evidence-based feedback loop between:

LABOUR MARKET
→ SKILLS
→ COURSES
→ TRAINING
→ EMPLOYERS
→ PLACEMENTS
→ FUTURE DEMAND

---

# 3. MAIN OBJECTIVE

Build a working web platform that continuously analyzes labour-market signals and converts them into actionable recommendations for:

1. Government
2. Training institutes
3. Employers
4. Students / candidates

The system should answer:

- What skills are currently in demand?
- Where are those skills demanded?
- Which skills are missing from existing courses?
- Which courses are outdated or oversupplied?
- Which skills will become important in the future?
- What should an institute change?
- What should the government invest in?
- What should a student learn?
- Why is a particular skill being recommended?
